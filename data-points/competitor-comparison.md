## PGKhata Advantages Over Competitors

### 1. **100% FREE** (vs ₹159–₹300/month)
No competitor offers free software. This is the #1 differentiator.

### 2. **Web-first** (vs mobile-first)
Most competitors are Android apps. PGKhata works on any device with a browser.

### 3. **Open source / Self-hosted**
No competitor offers this. Tech-savvy owners can host their own instance.

### 4. **Modern tech stack**
Next.js, TypeScript, proper testing. Competitors use legacy PHP/Java.

### 5. **Superior billing engine**
Line-item billing, configurable charge types, advance payments, auto-allocate — more sophisticated than any competitor.

### 6. **Approval workflow**
Both public signup AND manual tenant creation go through approval. Most competitors auto-activate.

### 7. **Financial safety nets**
Invoice voiding, promised payment date, checkout preview — features competitors lack.

### 8. **Granular permissions**
Module-level access control (tenants, billing, expenses, reports, structure) — only Niketan has this.
