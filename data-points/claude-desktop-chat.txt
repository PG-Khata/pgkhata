Architecture — kaise banega
Roles: Sirf admin (PG owner) ka login/dashboard hoga. Users ka koi login nahi — wo sirf WhatsApp/SMS pe notification receive karenge, koi web app access nahi. Ye actually achha design decision hai — simpler build, kam attack surface.

Backend (Express + Prisma + Postgres) — billing engine, cron job for 1st-of-month bill generation
Notification layer — WhatsApp (primary) + SMS (fallback if WhatsApp fails/undelivered)
Payment — Razorpay ya Cashfree se UPI QR/payment link generate karo, WhatsApp message mein bhej do
Billing flow (1st tareek):
Cron job → har tenant ke liye rent + electricity (meter reading admin dalega) + "other" bill calculate → Razorpay/Cashfree se payment link + UPI QR banao → WhatsApp template message bhejo (rent ₹X, electricity ₹Y, total ₹Z, pay via link) → payment webhook aane pe status "paid" mark ho jaye admin dashboard mein.

Weighed free-tier architecture tradeoffs and production reliability constraints
Weighed free-tier architecture tradeoffs and production reliability constraints
Haan, ye setup bilkul reasonable hai for starting — Vercel (frontend) + Render (backend) + uptime pinger, ye combo bahut log use karte hain MVP stage mein. Lekin kuch cheezein clearly bata deta hoon jo tera plan ko break kar sakti hain agar ignore kiya:

Jo sahi hai tere plan mein
Vercel for Next.js frontend — free tier generous hai, sleep issue hi nahi hota (serverless), 1000 users/10-20 RPM ke liye zero problem.
Render backend + UptimeRobot ping har 10-14 min — ye trick kaam karti hai. Render free service 15 min inactivity ke baad sleep karta hai, to agar tu 10 min interval pe ping maarta hai, wo sona hi nahi payega.
Load ke hisaab se (10-20 RPM, ~1000 users) — free tier ka CPU/RAM (512MB shared) technically handle kar lega. Yahan bottleneck load nahi, reliability hai.
Do cheezein jo problem denge
1. Render free hours budget tight hai
Render free plan sirf 750 instance-hours/month deta hai, sab free services milake. Ek service ko 24x7 zinda rakhne mein ~720-744 hours lag jaate hain — matlab lagbhag pura budget ek hi service khaa legi. Agar aage koi separate cron worker ya second service chalana ho, uske liye hours bachenge nahi. Isliye single backend service pe sab kuch (API + cron logic) rakhna better hoga.

2. Render ka free Postgres — ye sabse bada risk hai
Render ka free PostgreSQL database sirf 90 din tak rehta hai, uske baad automatically expire/delete ho jaata hai. Tere jaise billing app mein jahan real tenant data (phone, address proof, payment history) hai, ye unacceptable hai. Isliye database Render pe mat rakh — iske liye:

Supabase free tier ya Neon free tier use kar (dono persistent hain, expire nahi hote, generous free limits hain)
Backend (Render) sirf compute ke liye, DB alag jagah
Ek aur cheez soch — payment webhooks
Jab Render backend sleep se wake hota hai (cold start ~20-40 sec), aur usi waqt Razorpay/Cashfree ka payment webhook fire ho jaye, to delay ho sakta hai. Uptime ping isko mostly solve kar deta hai, but 100% guarantee nahi. Razorpay/Cashfree generally retry karte hain webhook delivery (multiple attempts), to real risk kam hai — bas test kar lena jab live jao.
