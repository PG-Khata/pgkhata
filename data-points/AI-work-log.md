# PGKhata AI work log

Purpose: preserve an honest record of how AI contributed, what the founder
decided, and what was later verified in the repository.

Status labels:

- **Verified** - supported by current source, a successful check, or a live
  integration result.
- **Chat-reported** - an AI tool said it completed the work, but this log does
  not treat that statement alone as proof.
- **Planned** - a decision or proposal that is not implemented.
- **Not implemented** - the current audit did not find a working path.
- **Blocked verification** - a check could not complete because of the
  environment or missing credentials/services.
