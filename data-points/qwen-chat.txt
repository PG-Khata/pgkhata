For a ₹499/month product targeting people who currently use *handwritten registers* and WhatsApp voice notes? ### 4. The "Electricity Calculation" Illusion

### The Verdict (The Constructive Part)
Despite the roast, **this is actually a very solid, viable micro-SaaS idea.** 
You are solving a real, painful, recurring problem. The "no app for tenant" rule is genius because tenant friction is the #1 reason these things fail. Your scope is beautifully restricted (no maintenance, no legal, just billing). 

**How to survive the roast:**
1. **Ditch the self-hosted WhatsApp bridge ASAP.** Bite the bullet and use official WhatsApp Business API (Gupshup, Twilio, or Interakt). The ₹0.50 per conversation cost is worth sleeping at night without fearing a ban.
2. **Simplify the tech stack.** You don’t need BullMQ yet. A simple cron job will do for 100 customers. Save the over-engineering for when you have 10,000 customers.
3. **GTM Pivot:** Instead of knocking on doors, stand *outside* a PG society or near a local kirana store that PG owners frequent, and just show them the WhatsApp message on your phone. *"Bhaiya, aapka bill aisa dikhega, 2 minute mein ban jayega."* Visual proof beats verbal demos for this demographic.
4. **Offer an annual plan.** ₹4,999/year. Get the cash upfront, reduce your monthly collection headache, and filter out the serious owners from the time-wasters.

You’re building a shovel for a gold rush that doesn’t know it needs shovels yet. Keep it stupid simple, fix the WhatsApp API risk, and go get those first 10 paying customers. You got this!
