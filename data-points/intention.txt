INTENTION.txt
Founder: Mukund Jha
Product: pgkhata.com, PG and hostel management

Why I am building this

PG owners across India are still running a genuinely hard, high-touch
business on WhatsApp messages, notebooks, and memory. Every month, the
same owner is manually typing out rent and electricity reminders to
twenty or thirty different tenants, one chat at a time, then trying to
remember who has actually paid. Tenant records, address proof, deposit
amounts, room history, all of it scattered across a register and a
phone gallery. This is not a small inconvenience. It is hours of
repeated, invisible work every single month, for a business that
already runs on thin margins.

I am not trying to fix everything wrong with the PG industry. I am not
solving vacancy, staff management, maintenance complaints, or the legal
ambiguity around tenancy status. Those are real problems, but they are
not mine to solve right now. I am solving two problems, and solving
them well: the chaos of manual rent collection, and tenant records that
live nowhere in particular.

What I intend to build

A tool that takes a PG owner from adding a tenant to sending a fully
itemised WhatsApp bill with a working UPI QR code, in minutes, without
asking the tenant to install anything or create any login. Nothing more
than that, until that part is genuinely solid.

How I intend to build it

Start small and honest. My MVP is deliberately narrow: tenant and room
management, one click billing, WhatsApp delivery, and a simple paid
versus pending view. Everything else, automated reminders, backups,
multi property support, a super admin layer, waits until the core is
proven with real owners paying real money for it.

I intend to keep costs close to zero for as long as possible, not
because I am afraid to spend, but because a product this early should
prove its value before it earns the right to cost more, from me or from
the owners using it.

How I intend to sell it

I am an introvert, and I am not going to pretend otherwise or force
myself into a sales personality that is not mine. Instead I intend to
lead with a working demo, not a pitch. I will walk into PG properties in
Noida myself, show the bill going out on WhatsApp in real time, and let
the product do the talking. I will be upfront about what it does and
does not do yet, because the fastest way to lose a PG owner's trust is
to promise a feature that does not exist.

What I will not do

I will not claim this solves problems it does not touch. I will not
chase every feature idea before the core loop is dependable. I will not
mistake a slow, local, word of mouth business for a failing one. This
is not a venture scale bet, and I do not need it to be one to make it
worth building.

What success looks like, honestly

A handful of PG owners in Noida who stop dreading the first of the
month, because pgkhata.com already did the part they used to do by hand.
That is the bar before anything else matters.

Scope reconciliation - 3 August 2026

The downloaded application has grown beyond this intended MVP. It now
contains multi-property support, subscriptions, automated billing code, and a
platform console, while the original WhatsApp plus UPI tenant-payment loop is
still incomplete. I will treat those extra modules as existing experiments,
not as proof that the core product is finished. My next decisions will be
based on repository evidence, security, and trials with real PG owners. This
note does not change the intention above; it records the gap between the
original scope and the generated implementation.
