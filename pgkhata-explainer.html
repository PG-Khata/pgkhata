What

What exactly is PGKhata?

PGKhata is a web-based software (like a website you open in your browser) designed specifically for PG (Paying Guest) owners in India. It helps you manage everything about your PG business — properties, tenants, rooms, beds, rent collection, expenses, staff, and communication — all from one dashboard.

Think of it as a digital manager for your PG business. Instead of maintaining registers, Excel sheets, or WhatsApp groups to track tenants and rent, PGKhata does it all automatically.

Where & When

Access and availability details

🌐
Where

Where does PGKhata run?

PGKhata is a cloud-based web application. You access it through your browser — no installation needed.

  • On your phone — Open Chrome/Safari and go to the website
  • On your laptop — Open any browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari)
  • On a tablet — Works on iPad and Android tablets too

The servers are hosted in Singapore for fast access from India. Your data is stored securely in PostgreSQL databases with encrypted connections.

🕐
When

When can I access it?

24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year.

  • Access your PG data anytime, from anywhere
  • Send bills and reminders even at midnight
  • Check reports on the go
  • No office hours restriction

Since it's a web app, there are no "business hours" — it's always available as long as you have internet.

📍
Where

Where is my data stored?

Your data is stored in:

  • PostgreSQL Database — Hosted on Neon (a managed database service). All tenant info, bills, payments, and settings live here.
  • Cloudflare R2 — For document storage (Aadhaar, PAN photos, etc.). S3-compatible, encrypted at rest.
  • Redis (Upstash) — For caching and background job queues (WhatsApp messages, etc.)

All connections are encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Your data is completely isolated from other PG owners — you can only see your own properties and tenants.

How It Works

Step-by-step guide to getting started

🚀
How

How do I get started?

Getting started takes less than 5 minutes:

  • Step 1: Go to the PGKhata website
  • Step 2: Click "Register" and create your account (email + password)
  • Step 3: Add your first property (name, address, city)
  • Step 4: Set up floors, rooms, and beds (or import from CSV)
  • Step 5: Add your tenants
  • Step 6: Start generating bills and collecting rent!
Quick setup: You can import your existing property structure and tenant list from a CSV file — no need to enter everything manually.
🧾
How

How does billing work?

PGKhata's billing system is automatic and flexible:

  • Auto-generation: Click "Generate Bills" and it creates bills for all tenants for the month
  • Line items: Each bill shows rent, electricity charges, and any other charges separately
  • Configurable charges: Add custom charge types (maintenance, food, laundry, etc.)
  • Rent plans: Set different rent amounts for different tenants
  • Electricity: Enter meter readings — the system calculates charges automatically
  • Late fees: Automatically apply late fees on overdue bills (configurable)
  • Invoice voiding: Made a mistake? Void the invoice and regenerate
  • Advance payments: Record advance payments and auto-allocate to future bills
📲
How

How does WhatsApp integration work?

PGKhata uses the official Meta WhatsApp Business API (not unofficial hacks that get banned):

  • Bill notifications: Send monthly bills to tenants on WhatsApp with one click
  • Payment reminders: Send reminders to tenants who haven't paid
  • Bulk reminders: Send reminders to ALL overdue tenants at once
  • Template messages: Pre-approved professional message templates
  • Notification preferences: Each tenant can choose WhatsApp, email, or both
Note: WhatsApp Business API requires template approval from Meta (takes 1-2 days). There's a small per-message cost (a few paise per message).
🔐
How

How does access control work?

PGKhata supports multiple users with different access levels:

  • Owner: Full access to everything — properties, billing, reports, staff management
  • Manager: Can be given access to specific modules only (e.g., only tenants and billing, no expenses)
  • Property scoping: Each staff member can be assigned to specific properties
  • Module permissions: Control access to individual modules (tenants, billing, expenses, reports, etc.)

This means you can give your accountant access to billing only, or let your property manager handle tenants without seeing financial reports.

📈
How

How do reports and analytics work?

PGKhata provides a comprehensive dashboard with:

  • Dashboard overview: Total income, expenses, outstanding amounts, occupancy rate
  • Monthly trend chart: Visual graph of income vs expenses over time
  • Due rent list: See who owes what and how overdue they are
  • Aging buckets: Outstanding amounts grouped by how long they've been overdue (0-30 days, 30-60 days, etc.)
  • Profit/Loss: See your net income after subtracting expenses
  • CSV export: Download any report as a spreadsheet for your accountant
🏗️
How

How is the property structure organized?

PGKhata uses a hierarchical structure that mirrors real PG buildings:

  • Property — Your PG building (e.g., "Sunshine PG, Koramangala")
  • Floor — Each floor of the building (Ground Floor, 1st Floor, 2nd Floor...)
  • Room — Rooms on each floor (Room 101, Room 102...)
  • Bed — Individual beds in each room (Bed A, Bed B, Bed C...)

Each bed can be tracked independently — you know exactly who is sleeping where, which beds are vacant, and the occupancy rate of each property.

How reliable is it?
